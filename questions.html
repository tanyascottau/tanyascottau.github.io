Book Club Questions

Stillwater

  1. How much did you know about Stillwater before reading it?
    Was it what you expected from the cover and back-cover blurb?

  2. Did the setting and atmosphere set a particular mood or tone? If so, how?

  3. Which character did you identify with the most?
    Were there any characters that felt particularly realistic or familiar?

  4. Did the main character, Luke, make any decisions that you didn't agree with?
    What informed his choices? Is violence ever acceptable?

  5. How was masculinity and the role of the father explored?
    Who were Luke's role models, and who did the best job?

  6. Luke and Emma come from very different backgrounds, but are they more alike than they seem?
    What do they have in common?

  7. What were the central themes of Stillwater? Which resonated most with you?

  8. Did any part of the story strike strong emotions in you, either positive or negative?
    Were any scenes difficult to read?

  9. Who do you think is the ideal reader for Stillwater? Who would you recommend it to?

  10. Were you satisfied with the ending? Did you have doubts or questions left unanswered?
    Can you imagine any ways the story might have ended differently?
